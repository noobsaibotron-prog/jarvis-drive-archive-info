Privacy notice

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Data and access

The owner chooses to connect their Google account. The archive component requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file, which allows access to files the app creates or that the owner explicitly makes available to it. The selected Markdown memories may contain personal information; their contents are backed up as provided.

How data is used and stored

The tool creates ZIP archives of selected local JARVIS memory files and stores them in the owner's Google Drive. It downloads archives to verify or restore them when requested. A local catalog tracks archive metadata. The app does not itself encrypt the archive with a separate user-held encryption key. Google stores the remote files according to the owner's account settings.

OAuth credentials are stored in the macOS Keychain. Temporary and cached archive copies are stored locally with restricted file permissions. The owner can remove the app's local cache using its provided command and manage remote files in Google Drive.

Sharing and control

The archive component does not intentionally publish or share the backup folder. The owner can manage files and sharing in Google Drive and revoke the app's access from their Google Account. Revocation does not automatically delete existing backup files.

The archive component does not use Google user data to train a machine-learning or AI model. This notice covers the archive component; other JARVIS features may use their own services and settings.

Use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
